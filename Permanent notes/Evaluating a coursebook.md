#U10
1. Is the material clearly structured - do you feel the order in which language items are presented is appropriate and logical?
2. Are the materials appropriately graded? Does the material get progressively more difficult at an appropriate speed?
3. Is there plenty of practice, recycling and revision?
4. Is there a teacher's book with lesson procedures, answers and extra teaching resources?
5. Does it provide exercises (in the book, workbook/CD, online) that the students can do independently?
6. Is the material attractively and clearly presented? (Avoid books in which pages look too cluttered.)
7. Is there a good balance of all four skills (reading, writing, listening and speaking) and language areas (vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation)?
8. Is there an interesting range of different activities?
9. Are the texts at the appropriate level for the students you are going to teach?
10. Are the listening and reading texts interesting and authentic looking/sounding?
11. If you are teaching in a non-Western country, is the material culturally appropriate? Is it assumed that the students will have certain cultural knowledge? Might it be offensive or irrelevant?
12. Does the book provide tests?
13. Will the school/the students/students' parents be able to afford this book?
14. Does the book provide grammar reference pages and vocabulary lists for students?
