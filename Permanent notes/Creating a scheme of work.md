#U10
Often based on a syllabus or exam, a scheme of week details the weekly content the teacher aims to cover. A good scheme of work should logically link different parts of the syllabus together. This makes the teaching feel more cohesive.

It may turn out that students find a certain topic more challenging or more interesting than you were expecting and you will need to adjust your scheme of work accordingly.

To ensure balance in your plan, use labels like: topic/vocabulary, function, grammar, pronunciation.
