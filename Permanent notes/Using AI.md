#### Lesson planning
You can use AI to outline a lesson for you, but always edit it after. Look out for:
- Incorrect timings
- Proper coverage (pronunciation, concept checking etc...)

#### Advice for prompt writing:
- Give a specific task
- Assign a role to the AI (you are an expert in.../ you are a kindergarten teacher)
- Outline audience
- Define tone and style
- Outline teaching approach, and learning objective(s)
- Give context (duration, number of students, students needs)
- Choose format for AI response
- Give example of what you want

PROVIDE CONTEXT/TONE IN PROMPT FIRST FOR BEST RESULTS!
